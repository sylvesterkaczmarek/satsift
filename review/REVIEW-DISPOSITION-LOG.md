## Completion rule

External technical qualification may be marked complete only when:

- at least one independent astrodynamics reviewer has completed `EXTERNAL-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md` against a recorded commit;
- all critical and major astrodynamics findings are resolved or explicitly accepted by that reviewer;
- manual keyboard and screen-reader testing has been completed against the normal path, error paths, chart alternatives and exports;
- all major accessibility blockers are resolved or explicitly accepted by the external tester;
- the final reviewed commit and dispositions are preserved here.
